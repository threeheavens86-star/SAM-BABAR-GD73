From Prince to Pharaoh

Born to King Philip II and Olympias. Tutored by the greatest mind of the age — Aristotle. Tamed the untamable horse Bucephalus at just 12 years old.

At 20, he inherited a kingdom and an army forged for greatness. By 32, he had conquered more territory than any man before him.
