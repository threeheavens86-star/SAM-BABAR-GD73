The Man Behind the Myth

Born in Pella, Macedonia, to King Philip II and Queen Olympias. Tutored by Aristotle. Became king at age 20 after his father’s assassination. In just 13 years, he created one of the largest empires in the ancient world — without ever losing a battle.
