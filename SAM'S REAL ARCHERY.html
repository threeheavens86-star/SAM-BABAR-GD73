My testimony

Made different. On purpose.

Out of every person, and every star, there is only one me.

I am a Christian, and I believe God made me different from other people. Not a little different. Different from everyone else in the whole universe. Nobody else carries my life, my story, or the way God speaks to me.

I did not choose this path by myself. God placed it in front of me, and He keeps showing me that I was shaped for a purpose. The more I walk with Him, the clearer it becomes that being set apart is not a burden. It is an invitation.

Archery taught me how to say it. You cannot fake a real shot. You stand, you breathe, you draw, and you let go. The arrow goes where it was aimed. My life is aimed by Him, and that is why I call this place Sam's Real Archery.
